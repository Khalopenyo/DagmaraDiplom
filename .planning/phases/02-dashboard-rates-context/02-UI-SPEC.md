# Phase 2 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

---

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|

| Preset | not applicable |

## Spacing Scale

Declared values (must be multiples of 4):

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| xs | 4px | Divider offsets, micro-gaps in labels |
| sm | 8px | Inline support copy and chip spacing |
| md | 16px | Default internal card rhythm |
| lg | 24px | Card padding and section spacing |
| xl | 32px | Major stack gaps between dashboard sections |
| 2xl | 48px | Separation between page intro and contextual surfaces |
| 3xl | 64px | Reserved only for oversized first-screen whitespace |

Exceptions: keep the shell exceptions from Phase 1: `80px` header height, `264px` sidebar width, `44px` minimum hit area for icon-only controls, `1200px` page max-width, `760px` narrative column.

---

## Typography

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 |
| Label | 14px | 600 | 1.4 |
| Heading | 20px | 600 | 1.2 |
| Display | 28px | 600 | 1.2 |

Additional rules:
- Account balance is the strongest dashboard datum and may scale to `36px / 600 / 1.1`.
- Rates values should read as financial data, not body copy: use semibold numeric emphasis without switching to a monospace font.
- Supporting text under cards stays muted and must never overpower the balance or China corridor row.

## Color

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | `#F5F1E8` | Page background and negative space around cards |
| Secondary (30%) | `#FFFFFF` | Account widget, quick-action cards, rates board, shell surfaces |
| Accent (10%) | `#0F6CBD` | Primary CTA, active quick action, key balance highlight, China corridor emphasis, helper badges |
| Destructive | `#C93C37` | Not used in Phase 2 beyond future-safe token continuity |

Accent reserved for: `Перейти к переводу`, account balance emphasis, active or primary quick-action treatment for `Переводы`, China corridor badge/marker, focus-visible states, shell badge `Simulated demo`. Do not tint all quick actions or all country rows blue.

## Layout Contract

- Phase 2 keeps the Phase 1 shell unchanged: sidebar, header, `1200px` page canvas, and `760px` content column are fixed inputs.
- `Главная` becomes a vertical stack of three surfaces: dashboard intro copy, primary account widget, and quick-actions grid.
- The account widget is the main anchor on the page; quick actions are secondary and must sit below it with enough whitespace to preserve hierarchy.
- `Обмен валют` becomes a compact rates board inside one main card, preceded by the existing inline simulation boundary.
- Rates rows should compare easily at a glance; avoid dense tables, sorting controls, filters, or financial-terminal styling.

---

## Dashboard Contract

- The first card on `Главная` is a read-only account widget for one seeded digital-ruble account.
- Card content order:
  1. owner name `Дагмара`
  2. masked account number `4756 •••• •••• 9018`
  3. balance `3 469.52 ЦР`
  4. one clear CTA `Перейти к переводу`
- The CTA may coexist with subtle helper copy, but the card must not introduce form fields, editable selectors, charts, or transaction ledgers.
- Quick actions render as eight evenly sized action tiles in a `2 x 4` grid on desktop.
- Only `Переводы` is visually primary and route-enabled; the remaining seven actions are display-first and must look intentionally inactive rather than broken.

Required quick-action labels:
- `Аккаунт и счета`
- `Переводы`
- `Снятие`
- `Оплата счета`
- `Накопления`
- `Кредитная карта`
- `Отчет о транзакциях`
- `Контакты`

## Rates Board Contract

- Rates board title remains `Обмен валют`.
- The board contains exactly three rows for Phase 2:
  - `Китай` — rate `2.234`
  - `Вьетнам` — rate `1.746`
  - `Южная Корея` — rate `5.151`
- Every row must show:
  - country marker (flag badge or equivalent)
  - country name
  - base unit `1 ЦР`
  - target simulated CBDC rate
- China row is visually emphasized as the primary corridor reference, but still clearly marked as simulated data.
- Vietnam and South Korea remain reference-only. No corridor switching, row expansion, or drill-down interaction appears in Phase 2.

---

## Interaction Contract

- `Перейти к переводу` on the account widget routes to `/transfers`.
- Quick-action tile `Переводы` also routes to `/transfers`.
- The other seven quick-action tiles are non-destructive display surfaces. They may show hover/focus styling only if that styling does not imply a fully implemented workflow.
- Rates rows are read-only in Phase 2. Hover is allowed for polish, but selection, sorting, and editing are not.
- The persistent `Simulated demo` badge and canonical boundary copy remain visible and unchanged from Phase 1.

---

## Visual Hierarchy Contract

- On `Главная`, the balance card outranks every other surface.
- The dashboard intro copy should frame the account widget, not compete with it.
- `Переводы` quick action is the single most important action tile and may receive accent emphasis; the remaining tiles stay neutral.
- On `Обмен валют`, the China row is the first visual anchor inside the board, followed by the remaining reference rows.
- Phase 2 must still feel like one guided demo story, not a generic fintech portal with many equally weighted choices.

---

## Anti-Patterns

- No charts, sparklines, or pseudo-banking analytics on the dashboard.
- No generic enterprise table controls on the rates board.
- No live-refresh timestamps, websocket-shaped language, or claims about official central-bank connectivity.
- No secondary CTA set that creates competing flows from the dashboard.
- No visual treatment that makes Vietnam or South Korea look executable in the scripted v1 corridor.
