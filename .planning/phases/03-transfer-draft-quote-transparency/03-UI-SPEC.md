## Layout Contract

- Phase 3 keeps the Phase 1 shell unchanged: sidebar, header, `1200px` page canvas, and centered content remain fixed inputs.
- `Переводы` keeps the intro-card simulation boundary, then becomes a two-column desktop layout:
  - left column: editable transfer form card(s)
  - right column: sticky or visually persistent quote preview card
- The preferred desktop split is asymmetrical, with the form column slightly wider than the quote column.
- Below laptop-width breakpoints, the quote card may fall below the form, but the quote must remain visible without opening a modal or step transition.
- Do not turn the route into a wizard, drawer flow, or nested route tree in Phase 3.

---

## Transfer Draft Contract

- The first editable block is `Счет списания`, showing one seeded Dagmara digital-ruble account and the available balance `3 469.52 ЦР`.
- The route must show a transfer-type switch with exactly two options:
  - `По номеру карты`
  - `По номеру телефона`
- The recipient area must contain:
  - one visible favorites strip with `Emma`, `Justin`, and `+`
  - one editable identifier field whose label and placeholder follow the active transfer mode
- Required identifier labels:
  - card mode: `Номер карты получателя`
  - phone mode: `Номер телефона получателя`
- The debit amount field label is `Сумма списания`; it is the only editable monetary source of truth.

---

## Quote Preview Contract

- The quote card must display a visible simulated-corridor badge for `Россия → Китай`.
- Quote detail rows must appear before confirmation:
  - `Курс`
  - `Сумма списания`
  - `Сумма получения`
  - `Комиссия платформы`
  - `Итого`
- The fixed rate line must read exactly `1 ЦР = 2.234 ЦЮ`.
- Platform fee stays deterministic and flat at `10 ₽`.
- The main derived recipient amount is displayed in `¥` and updates immediately when the debit amount changes.
- `Подтвердить` lives inside the quote card and reads as the end of the draft phase, not as a live settlement action.

---

## Interaction Contract

- Selecting `По номеру карты` or `По номеру телефона` updates the active segment, recipient-field label, placeholder, and validation rule.
- Clicking `Emma` or `Justin` highlights the chosen chip and may prefill the current identifier field, but the field remains editable.
- Clicking `+` does not open a new flow in Phase 3; it is a display-first affordance only.
- Entering a debit amount recalculates the derived recipient amount, fee line, and total immediately.
- `Подтвердить` is disabled when:
  - recipient identifier is empty
  - identifier format does not match the active mode
  - debit amount is empty, zero, or negative
  - debit amount exceeds the available balance
- Clicking enabled `Подтвердить` in Phase 3 does not yet navigate to tracker/receipt routes.

---

## Visual Hierarchy Contract

- The form card is the primary editing surface.
- The quote card is the primary confirmation surface and must stay visible while editing.
- The debit amount input and the derived recipient amount are the two strongest numbers on the page.
- The favorites strip is supportive context and must not visually outrank the transfer-type selector or quote card.
- Validation copy should read as precise guidance, not as punitive error banners.

## Anti-Patterns

- No multi-step wizard or progress tracker on the transfer page.
- No country picker, corridor selector, or alternative route chooser in Phase 3.
- No fake live-rate spinner, refresh timestamp, or "best rate" marketing copy.
- No receipt-like surface or transaction ID preview before actual Phase 4 submission.
- No blockchain hashes, block metaphors, or generic crypto-wallet visuals.
